Privacy Policy for Earful

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data collection (none)

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any recordings, personal data, or device information. We have no servers and no accounts.

2. Local storage only

Your recordings, bookmarks, notes, transcripts, folders and settings are stored only on your own device: the audio files in the app's private storage, and the rest in a local SQLite database (via the Drift library). No other app can read them. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so nothing is copied to Google's cloud or to a new phone unless you choose to, as described in section 7.

3. The microphone and notifications

Earful uses the microphone only to record when you tap Record. While a recording is running, Android shows a notification that Earful is using the microphone. When you play a recording, Earful shows a playback notification with lock-screen controls so it can keep playing with the screen off; it shows only the recording's title.

4. No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, fingerprinting, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any tracking technology. The app has no internet permission of its own and makes no network requests.

5. Third-party services

The only external service is Google Play, which processes your Earful Pro subscription through the Google Play Billing Library. Starting a subscription, restoring it, managing it and checking that it is still active go through it: at start-up and when you come back to the app, Earful asks Google Play whether your subscription is active, and remembers on your phone only that it was, and when. We do not receive or store your payment details or identity: those are handled entirely by Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that part. The app has no internet permission of its own.

6. Things you start that leave the app

The app never sends anything by itself. These hand over to another app only when you choose: sharing a recording, a transcript or notes (to the sharing app you pick), and rating the app (four or five stars open the Google Play page so you can leave a public review; a lower rating with a comment opens an email draft to the developer, and the comment is only sent if you send that email, so please do not put anything private from your recordings in it). The optional home screen widget only opens Earful. The optional daily journal reminder is a notification made by your phone itself at the time you choose; it needs no network. The app is shown in English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, Spanish, French and Portuguese; this policy and the terms are in English. The app lock uses the phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN prompt: Earful never sees your biometric data, only whether you passed. "Hide Earful in recent apps" keeps the app out of the recent-apps list and screenshots.

7. Your own cloud (optional)

Earful never connects to the internet. If you want a copy of your recordings somewhere else, choose a folder in Settings > Your cloud. Earful then copies your audio, transcripts and notes into that folder as ordinary files (audio files, text and a library file in a readable format) and can bring them back on this or another phone. The folder is yours: it can be on the phone, on a memory card, or inside the synced folder of a cloud app you already use, such as Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive. If it is a cloud folder, that cloud app, not Earful, uploads the files, and its own privacy policy applies. Earful holds only the folder you chose (through Android's folder access) and nothing else. If you switch on "Encrypt backups", Earful asks for a passphrase and encrypts every file before it is copied (AES-256 with a key made from the passphrase); the folder then holds only unreadable files, and a small file with the settings needed to check the passphrase (never the passphrase itself). Earful keeps the key, not the passphrase, on your phone so backups can run by themselves. If you forget the passphrase, nobody, including us, can open those backups. You can stop using the folder at any time; files already copied stay there until you delete them.

8. Transcripts, translation and notes

Transcripts are made on your phone by a speech model that ships inside Earful (a small Whisper model, licensed under the MIT licence). The audio is not sent anywhere to be transcribed or translated: there is no network use at all. Highlights, action items, study notes, flashcards and answers to your questions are worked out on your phone from the transcript. Telling the voices apart and finding highlights and action items are also worked out on your phone. If you turn on "Remember voices" (it is off until you do), then when you name a speaker Earful keeps a short list of numbers that describes how that voice sounds, together with the name, so it can name the same voice in other recordings. It is not a recording of the voice, stays on your phone, and is never sent anywhere. You can forget any voice, or turn the setting off to forget all of them, in Settings, Known voices. "Add to calendar" opens your calendar app with an event filled in; you save it there, and Earful asks for no calendar permission. Optionally you can have a larger, more accurate Whisper model (Base or Small) delivered by Google Play. Google Play, not Earful, downloads it (Earful has no internet access of its own); once downloaded it works exactly like the built-in model, on your phone. Transcripts may contain mistakes, particularly with noise, accents and technical words: check anything important against the recording.

9. Adding audio

You can add sound files from your phone or from other apps (for example a WhatsApp voice note shared to Earful). Earful copies the file into its private storage and does not change or send the original. Earful asks for no access to your contacts, location, camera or other files.

10. Children's privacy

Earful is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age. Consistent with Section 1, we collect no data at all.

11. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / POPIA)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Everything is under your physical control on your device. To delete it, delete recordings inside the app, use "Clear all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app.

12. Recording other people

You are responsible for recording people lawfully. Many places require the consent of the people you record, or of at least one of them, and some keep recordings of private conversations restricted. Tell people you are recording, and keep what you record safe. If you share a recording, you are the one sharing it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy we will flag it clearly in the app. This version is valid for the fully offline version of the app.
